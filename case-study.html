The challenge

Saturating local markets with manual sales reliance

RO-PARTS faced a hard growth ceiling. Their wholesale model relied heavily on physical account managers calling on regional mechanics. Organic digital search traffic was flat, and digital ad funnels generated generic retail clicks instead of corporate wholesale contract leads.

Critical roadblocks identified:

- 82% of digital ad spend resulted in single-item consumers rather than fleet accounts. - No connection between CRM data and paid media optimization algorithms. - High competitive pressure from central European conglomerates entering Romania.

Building an automated database-first pipeline
